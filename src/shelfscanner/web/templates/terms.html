What ShelfScanner is

ShelfScanner reads the books in a photo of a shelf and recommends some of them to you based on the preferences you give it. It is free, and it is a hobby project run by one person.

Recommendations

The books are read and chosen by AI models. They can misread a spine, invent a title, or recommend a book you will not like. Treat the picks as suggestions, not facts, and check a book before you buy or borrow it.

What you upload

Upload only photos you have the right to use. Do not upload photos of people, documents or anything that is not a bookshelf. By uploading a photo you allow us to process it to produce recommendations and to keep it as the privacy policy says.
