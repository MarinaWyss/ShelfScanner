Find your next book on a shelf that is already in front of you.

Photograph a bookshelf at a shop, a library or a friend's place. ShelfScanner reads the spines, checks the titles, and picks five books from that shelf for you, with a reason for each.

Scan a shelf

How it works

  1. Photograph the shelf. One photo of the whole shelf, spines facing you. It is resized on your phone before it is sent.
  2. The books are read and checked. A vision model reads the titles off the spines, and each one is checked against a book catalogue so nothing gets made up.
  3. Five are picked for you. Your genres, favourite authors and, if you upload it, your Goodreads history choose five from that shelf, with a reason for each. Save the ones you want; mark the ones you don't.

What is kept, and what is not

  • No account. This device is remembered with a cookie, and your saved books live with it.
  • The photo is stored privately for the scan and deleted after thirty days. Its location and camera data are stripped before it leaves your phone.
  • A Goodreads export is read once to build your preferences and is not stored.
  • Ten scans per device per hour, so the service stays up for everyone.
