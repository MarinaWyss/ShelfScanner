# Sourced shelf photos: attribution

Every photo in the `sourced` set (change 006), with the attribution its licence asks for.
The images themselves are not in the repository; `uv run shelfscanner photos fetch` downloads
them from `source.url` in each label file. Openverse rows are Wikimedia Commons files found
through the Openverse API; the link goes to the Commons file page in both cases.

Selection: CC0, public domain or CC BY only; long edge at least 1200 px; no readable face of a
person present in the photo (faces printed on book covers are not people); at least about eight
legible spines when viewed at 1600 px.
