## What the rows say

Written by the lead on 2026-09-03 to seed the routine; the window covers the
MVP and wave 1 and 2 work, so most rows are test runs, not use.

- **App, failovers to the fallbacks (reading rows 50, 188, 189; choosing
  rows 50, 51).** Configuration, not model failure. The reading and
  choosing primaries (Gemini 3.8 Flash, GPT-5.4 mini) have no key in
  `.env` yet; failover did its job and every scan completed on Sonnet and
  Haiku. Noise until the keys arrive; the pattern should disappear the day
  they do, and the dashboard's failover count is the check.
- **App, choosing row 50 (Qwen 3.8 Flash, `http 429`).** Model-provider
  failure on OpenRouter's shared pool, the second in a day. Already acted
  on: the choosing fallback moved from Qwen to Haiku on 2026-09-03
  (config/models.toml). No photo to add; nothing was wrong with the reply.
- **App, not for me on recommendation 51 pick 1 (*Stranger in a Strange
  Land*).** One mark, one reader. The reason the model gave fits the stated
  preferences (psychology, conceptual science fiction), so this is taste,
  not a model failure. Watch the title, not the row.
- **Test set, `truncated` on Qwen (reading rows 23, 24, 27) and Sonnet
  (row 94).** Model failure with one cause: reasoning tokens consumed the
  4,096-token reply budget on dense shelves. Already acted on in 002:
  `DEFAULT_MAX_TOKENS` is 8,192 and the rows since do not truncate. Row 94's
  photo (a dense shelf) is already labelled; nothing to add.
- **Test set, Qwen `parse` (row 28) and `http 429` (choosing row 28).** The
  parse error is an empty reply from the same rate-limited pool; same
  family as the 429s above, same fix.
- **Test set, failover row 44.** The Gemini key again; see the first item.
- **No application failures in either population.** Every photo stored
  reached a model.

## Suggested change

None this week. Both repeated patterns are already closed: the truncations
by the larger reply budget (002), the rate limits by dropping Qwen as the
choosing fallback. Two things to watch in the next review rather than
change now:

1. If `http 429` from OpenRouter appears again on any model after the
   fallback change, propose removing OpenRouter models from the stage
   tables entirely and keeping the adapter for research runs only.
2. If the Gemini and OpenAI failovers are still present once the keys are
   in `.env`, that is a bug, not configuration; look at the adapters' key
   loading.
