**1. The reading primary has no credit (affects `model-router.md`,
`monitoring.md`, `config/models.toml`).**

- *What is wrong.* The Gemini project's prepaid credit has run out. Every
  reading call tries Gemini, fails, and fails over to Sonnet.
- *How the rows show it.* 22 app failovers and 692 test-set failovers
  with "prepayment credits are depleted", from 2026-09-08 to today, under
  two status codes. `research.check` fails on reading cost and cost per
  scan.
- *What to change.* First a decision, not code: top up the Google
  project, or make Sonnet the reading primary in `config/models.toml` and
  accept its cost (002 D6: run `research.check` and record the new
  baseline). Second, small and separate: give an exhausted-credit reply
  its own error head (for example `quota`) instead of the provider's
  status code. Then the review shows it as one group, and the admin page
  could flag it the way it flags stale prices.
- *How we would know.* The next review shows no credit failovers, and
  `research.check` passes on cost against whichever baseline is chosen.

**2. Short shelves get five picks anyway (affects `recommendation.md`,
`prompts/`, the test set).**

- *What is wrong.* With fewer than five titles, GPT-5.4 mini returns five
  picks. The prompt's last line, "check each of your five titles",
  contradicts its own rule for short lists.
- *How the rows show it.* Recommendation rows 174, 175 (4 titles, 5
  picks) and 238, 239 (3 titles, 5 picks), ten days apart. Recommendation
  294 is the same pressure in a softer form: three reasons concede the
  book does not fit, written to fill five slots.
- *What to change.* A `recommend_v7` that is v6 with the closing check
  reworded to "each of your titles" (five, or all of them if the list is
  shorter). Add the short-shelf photos from rows 174 and 238 to the test
  set with labels, so the wrong count is checked before a prompt ships.
  Whether a reason may say plainly that a pick is a weak fit, instead of
  stretching, is a separate product question for Marina.
- *How we would know.* On the short-shelf test photos, no row has
  `expected N recommendations`. In the app, the next reviews show no
  wrong-count group. `research.check` on the core set does not regress on
  on-list or overlap.
