## Suggested change

Two patterns repeat. Each needs a proposal in `docs/changes/` and Marina's
yes. Nothing here changes code.

**1. Ask for the right number of picks (affects `docs/specs/recommendation.md`).**
*What is wrong:* when fewer than five titles survive the check,
GPT-5.4 mini repeats picks to reach five, and the count check rejects the
whole scan. *How the rows show it:* 14 errors in this window, two in
September (174, 175, 238, 239) and ten in November, on 7 photos in 5
sessions, and none of them showed the reader any picks. *What to change:*
a `recommend_v7` that puts the number in the text ("Recommend exactly
{n} different books", with n = min(5, the number of shelf titles)) and drops
the fixed "five" from the check line. Also pass `maxItems` = n in
`RECOMMENDATIONS_SCHEMA` for adapters that enforce it. Decide in the
proposal whether exact duplicates should be dropped before the count check:
that would rescue these replies, but it changes the spec's rule. *How we
would know:* photo 308 added to the test set with its labels passes under
`research.check` with no count error, the core five do not regress (002 D6),
and the next review shows no `expected N recommendations` rows.

**2. Gemini credit, and the review tool that hid it (affects
`docs/specs/model-router.md` and `docs/specs/monitoring.md`).**
*What is wrong:* the reading primary has been unusable for about four weeks,
twice in this window, because the Google prepayment ran out. Nothing alerts
on it (scoping section 7: no alerting), and this week's draft labelled most
of it `primary failed` because of the 1,000-row cap. *How the rows show it:*
2,641 failovers whose `failover_error` is "prepayment credits are depleted",
and 4 Gemini readings since 2026-10-31. *What to change:* first, a decision
rather than code: top up or auto-renew the Google credit, or make Sonnet
the primary on purpose and record that in `config/models.toml`. Second, a
small fix to `research.review.fetch_failover_errors`: page the `.in_()`
read in chunks, as `metrics.fetch` already does, so the draft groups every
failover by its real error. Optionally, the admin page could flag a stage
whose failover share over the window is above, say, 50%. *How we would
know:* the next draft has no `primary failed` group, and the reading
failover count drops back to single figures once the credit is restored,
or is expected and recorded if Sonnet becomes the primary.
