## Suggested change

Three suggestions, most important first. Each needs a proposal in
`docs/changes/` and Marina's yes. Nothing here is approved.

1. **The choosing prompt asks for a fixed five even when the shelf does not
   allow it.** *What is wrong:* `recommend_v6` says "exactly five" twice and
   gives the short-list exception once, in between. GPT-5.4 mini follows the
   "five". *How the rows show it:* 10 wrong-count rows on two dates seven
   weeks apart (238/239, 557/559/560 and five more), each one a failed scan
   for the reader. Recommendation 294 shows the same pressure from the other
   side: four stretched "self-help" picks, four marks. *What to change:* a
   `recommend_v7` that states the count in the input ("The list has N books;
   recommend min(5, N)") and changes the closing self-check to "your picks"
   rather than "your five". Whether a weak shelf may return fewer than five
   picks is a product decision for Marina. If she says yes, the count check
   in `recommend.py` changes with it. *Spec affected:* `recommendation.md`
   (Input to the model, Checks) and `web.md` ("choosing five for you").
   *How we would know:* the photos behind 238/239 and 557 to 560, added to
   the test set with labels, return the right count on GPT-5.4 mini.
   `research.check` shows no regression on on-list or overlap. The next
   review has no `expected N recommendations` rows.
2. **The reading primary can be out of service for days without anyone
   seeing it.** *How the rows show it:* this is the second review in a row
   where Gemini's failovers are a configuration or billing problem: keys
   missing in the last review, prepaid credits depleted twice in this one,
   with 991 rows between the `http 402` and `http 429` groups. *What to
   change:* first, a non-code step: enable auto-recharge or raise the Google
   prepayment. Second, have the nightly eval report how many readings the
   primary itself served, and treat "primary served under half the photos"
   as a failed check rather than comparing stale rows. *Spec affected:*
   `model-router.md` (failover) and `monitoring.md`. *How we would know:* the
   next review has no `http 402` group, and a night with depleted credits
   turns the nightly eval red.
3. **The draft loses the primary's error past 1,000 failovers.** *What is
   wrong:* `research/review.py:fetch_failover_errors` does not page, so 178
   failovers in this draft show up as "primary failed". *What to change:*
   page that read in chunks, the way `metrics.fetch` does. Shorter windows
   also help: the review did not run for eight weeks (check the
   `WEEKLY_REVIEW` variable). *Spec affected:* `monitoring.md`, "The weekly
   review". *How we would know:* a test in `tests/test_review.py` with more
   than 1,000 seeded failovers, and no "primary failed" group in the next
   draft.

On the watch list, not to change yet: Gemini `truncated` at 8,192 tokens
with more than 4,800 of them spent reasoning (3 rows in two months). If it
reaches three in one weekly window, propose setting Gemini's reasoning
effort the way Qwen's is set in `config/models.toml`.
