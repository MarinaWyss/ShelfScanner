## Suggested change

Two patterns repeat. Neither one is approved by this file. Each needs a
proposal in `docs/changes/` and Marina's yes.

**1. Short shelves get five picks or none (recommendation.md).**
*What is wrong:* when the verified list has fewer than five titles,
GPT-5.4 mini returns five, the count check fails, and the reader gets no
picks. Both scans on 2026-09-05 and 2026-09-15 ended this way, two rows
each. The same pressure to reach five shows up in recommendation 294:
the model padded a shelf that barely matched with three picks whose
reasons admit they don't fit, and the reader marked four of the five.
*What to change:* a `recommend_v7` prompt that says the count once and
only once, "five books, or every book on the list if it has fewer", and
replaces "check each of your five titles" with "check each of your
titles". Add a labelled short-list photo (the 2026-09-05 or 2026-09-15
scan) to the test set so `research.check` exercises it. Whether the
chooser may also return fewer than five when fewer fit the preferences
is a product question for Marina. It would change the spec's "picks
five" and the save-rate denominator, so it should be a separate
proposal if she wants it.
*How we'd know:* `research.check` passes against the baseline (002 D6),
the short-list photo returns the right count across a matrix run, and
the next reviews show no `expected N recommendations, got 5` rows.

**2. Test runs and the app share one Gemini prepaid balance
(model-router.md, deployment.md).**
*What is wrong:* the reading primary's Google account ran out of
prepaid credit around 2026-09-08. Every Gemini call then failed over:
14 app scans and 480 test-set rows. Fallback kept scans working but
cost more. The nightly eval reads `secrets.GEMINI_API_KEY`. If that is
the same key and project the app uses, the test set's volume drains the
app's balance with nothing to warn about it. Scoping section 7 says
there is no alerting, so the review is the first place this showed up.
*What to change:* first check whether the two keys are the same. If they
are, give research runs (nightly, matrix) their own Google project and
key, and switch the app's project to auto-reload or a monthly budget
instead of prepaid credit. This is configuration, not code. The
proposal should still record the decision in deployment.md, and in
model-router.md if the failover path should label `RESOURCE_EXHAUSTED`
as a billing error in its own right rather than a plain `http 429`.
*How we'd know:* no `RESOURCE_EXHAUSTED` failovers in the next reviews,
and the report's Sonnet failover count for reading stays near zero
while the nightly runs.
