## Suggested change

**Make a primary that never answers visible, and stop the gate passing on stale
rows.**

*What is wrong.* For two review windows the reading primary has not answered a
call. Last week the key was missing; this week the key is there and the account
is out of credit. Failover works exactly as specified, and that is the problem:
nothing fails, no error row is written, the dashboard's error columns stay
clean, and the only trace is a failover count that has been non-zero since the
project began. Meanwhile every read runs on the fallback, and
`research.check` — the acceptance gate for any model, prompt or adapter change
(002 D6) — reports the primary's recall from rows written before the outage
started.

*How the rows show it.* The Patterns list: 11 app rows and 240 test-set rows of
`http 429` on `google/gemini-3.8-flash`, none of them a model failure and so
none of them in an errors column. The report's reading table: the Sonnet line
at a 1,568-pixel edge is 65 photos with 65 failovers, recall 0.75 and 1.6
invented titles per photo at $0.0105 a read, against Gemini's 1.00, 0.0 and
$0.0076 — not a like-for-like photo set, but the fallback is neither as
accurate nor as cheap as the model it stands in for. And `research.check` today
prints `reading google/gemini-3.8-flash recall 1.0000 ... rows 5, errors 0`:
those five rows are the OpenRouter rows from 001, since no row from the direct
`google` adapter appears in the report at all.

*What to change.* Two parts. The first is not code: top up the Google credits.
The second is, and is worth doing anyway, because the next outage will announce
itself no better than this one did.

1. `research.check` refuses to pass a stage on stale rows: when the newest row
   for a stage primary is older than the run by more than a set number of days,
   or predates the adapter named in `config/models.toml`, it fails with the
   model and the date named instead of printing a green number. Spec:
   `docs/specs/model-router.md` (failover and the check).
2. The admin page and the review draft each gain one figure per stage: the
   share of the window's rows the primary answered. A primary at zero goes into
   "Patterns" with its error, the way an error group does. Spec:
   `docs/specs/monitoring.md` (the metrics, and the draft's Patterns).

*How we would know it worked.* With the credits still out, `research.check`
would have failed on 2026-09-07 naming `google/gemini-3.8-flash` and the date of
its newest row, rather than passing; and this draft's Patterns would have led
with the reading primary answering none of the sixteen app scans, instead of a
429 count inside a failover table. Once the credits are back, the next review's
reading failovers fall to near zero and the app's `extractions.model` values
carry `google/gemini-3.8-flash` again.

This is a suggestion, not approval: a change needs a proposal in
`docs/changes/` and Marina's yes (009 D2).
