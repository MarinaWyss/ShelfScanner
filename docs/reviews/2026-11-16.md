## Suggested change

Two patterns repeat. Each needs its own proposal and Marina's yes, and
there is one action that needs no code.

1. **Short lists: GPT-5.4 mini repeats picks to reach five
   (`docs/specs/recommendation.md`).**
   - *What is wrong:* when checking keeps fewer than five titles, the
     choosing model fills the reply to five with duplicates. The count
     check rejects it, and the reader sees "Choosing failed" on a shelf
     that had good answers.
   - *How the rows show it:* 12 app rows over four separate days (174,
     175, 238, 239, 552, 553, 556, 557, 559, 560, 650, 651). Every one is
     a three- or four-title list with duplicates.
     It is the largest single cause of incomplete app scans.
   - *What to change:* give the model the number, not the rule. Have
     `input_text` state "recommend exactly N" with N = min(5, kept titles)
     in a prompt v7, and set the schema's `maxItems` to N. A second option
     to weigh in the proposal is to drop exact duplicates before the count
     check, so a reply with N distinct shelf picks plus repeats is kept.
   - *How we would know:* promote photos 171, 265 and 308 into the test
     set. The short-list photos must then produce zero wrong-count rows,
     `research.check` must pass on the five core photos, and the next
     review must show no `expected N recommendations` rows.
2. **The review mislabels failovers when there are more than about a
   thousand (`docs/specs/monitoring.md`, "The weekly review").**
   - *What is wrong:* `fetch_failover_errors` reads every failover id in
     one `.in_()` query. Past roughly 1,000 rows the rest come back
     unlabelled and are grouped as `primary failed`, as 990 rows were
     here.
   - *What to change:* read in chunks, as `metrics.fetch` already pages.
     Also consider grouping failovers on the error text after the status,
     so Google's 402 and 429 "credits depleted" fall into one group.
   - *How we would know:* a `tests/test_review.py` case with more than
     1,000 seeded failover rows, and no `primary failed` group in the next
     draft.
